ENGINEERING TOOLKIT

Cylinder force calculator

Theoretical force before friction, back pressure and other losses.

How the calculation works

F = p × A. Convert bar to N/mm² by multiplying by 0.1. Extension area = πD²/4. Retraction area = π(D² − d²)/4.

For 6 bar, a 32 mm bore and 12 mm rod: extension ≈ 482.5 N and retraction ≈ 414.7 N. These are theoretical forces. Friction, exhaust back pressure and other losses reduce usable force.

HYDRAULIC SYSTEM SIZING

Power, force & speed

Ideal actuator force assumes negligible opposing chamber pressure/friction. v = Q/A, with Q converted from L/min to mm³/s. Pfluid = Δp × Q / 600 kW; Pshaft = Pfluid/η. Retraction cap-end return flow can exceed pump flow. This is steady sizing, not transient simulation.

ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Three-phase motor current

Iline = Pshaft × 1000 / (√3 × Vline × PF × η). Balanced sinusoidal steady operation assumed. This does not select cables, overload settings, breakers or VFD input ratings. Confirm nameplate data and protection coordination.

IEC 61131-3 / CODESYS WORKBENCH

Ladder programming & scan lab

Edit rungs, compile, then step the program. Contacts use TRUE/FALSE tests; parallel branches mean OR and series contacts mean AND. Physical stop/overload inputs below are TRUE when healthy.

Equivalent Structured Text

Generated for CODESYS-style IEC boolean logic and TON instances. Paste declarations and implementation into a new standard PLC_PRG POU; map inputs separately. Download a fuller state-machine example. Neither example has been compiled in CODESYS here.

Teaching runtime: boolean tags, AND/OR/NOT, COIL, SET, RESET and non-retentive TON only. It is not a general ST interpreter or a PLC emulator. Run advances one simulated scan every 100 ms of wall time; the configured scan interval controls simulated time. Green paths show the last executed scan. Inputs are sampled once per simulated scan; rungs execute top-to-bottom. The first TRUE call starts a timer at ET=0. Runtime state resets when you compile or load a project; your program and inputs are saved.
Ordinary PLC permissive logic is not a safety-rated stop function. There is no connection to machinery.

Engineering symbol library
Original vector drawings use conventional ISO 1219 fluid-power and IEC-style electrical/ladder notation. They replace decorative component icons in the studio and show state boxes, flow directions, actuation and contact logic. They are educational drawings, not a certified standards library or complete production CAD schematics.
Reference standards: ISO 1219-1/-2, IEC 60617 and IEC 61131-3. Consult the applicable edition and project drawing conventions. Further reading: ISO, IEC symbol database, CODESYS documentation.
